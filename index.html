Strategy notes for IDX investors

Dollar-cost averaging: invest the same amount every month regardless of market moves — it removes timing stress and suits a salary cycle.
Blue chips (LQ45/IDX30): core holdings like banking and consumer staples give stability and liquidity.
Dividend stocks: reinvest dividends to compound; look for consistent payout history.
Reksadana indeks / ETF: lowest-effort diversification; good for the portion you don't want to research.
Cash buffer: keep an emergency fund (3–6 months of expenses) in RDPU or deposito before going heavier into stocks.
